    Recent Business Activity

    Latest recorded business events. “View” shows the source transaction and its accounting effect; loan records also show liquidation/allocation and summary.

    Cash Transactions

    Record manual cash movements — expenses, owner capital, loans and drawings — outside the linked sales, purchase and collection modules.
    Use Sales, Purchases and Collections modules for linked business events. Manual cash is for legitimate items such as expenses, owner capital, loans, drawings and other cash movements.

    Inventory

    Products with their lot-level stock and expiry, the expired-stock approval queue, manual spoilage and stocktake snapshots — valued at weighted-average cost.
    Inventory uses historical transaction costs and weighted-average costing for new purchases and sales. Inventory value is traceable to the movement ledger.
    Expiry is tracked per lot / batch, never on the product master. Sales consume stock FEFO (First Expire, First Out), and expired lots are blocked from sales until they are approved in the Queue tab. Click a product row’s Lots button to see every batch, its expiry date and its status.

    Automatic Spoilage / Wastage Queue

    Ang system automatic nga mo-detect og expired nga lot ug mo-text sa owner. Walay ma-minus sa stock ug walay accounting entry hangtod nga i-approve nimo diri.
    This is the only place expired stock is written off. Approving an item reduces inventory at weighted-average cost and posts Dr Spoilage / Wastage Expense, Cr Inventory. Dismissing an item closes the request without touching stock or the books.

    Manual Spoilage / Wastage

    Use Manual Spoilage / Wastage for physical losses such as damage, rot, wilt, overripe stock, dropped, contaminated or otherwise unsellable inventory. Expired stock is handled through the Automatic Spoilage / Wastage Queue.
    Kung madaot, malata, malaya, o dili na baligya ang prutas/gulay, i-record diri. Ang system mo-minus sa inventory gamit ang weighted-average cost ug mo-record og separate Spoilage / Wastage Expense. Ang expired nga lot, didto sa Queue i-approve.
    Ang spoilage dili i-count as sales ug dili pud siya i-add sa cash outflow. Inventory loss siya nga na-convert ngadto sa Spoilage / Wastage Expense.

    System Stocktake Snapshot

    Ang snapshot mo-record sa system quantity. I-butang ang actual nga na-count sa ubos aron ma-reconcile ang physical stock ug automatic nga mag-post ang variance adjustment.

    Customer Collections / Receivables

    Apply customer payments against credit sales. Collections reduce Accounts Receivable and never create new revenue.
    A collection reduces Accounts Receivable; it does NOT create new revenue. Link a collection to an actual credit sale whenever possible.

    Supplier Payment / Accounts Payable

    Pay suppliers you owe. Every recorded payment is Cash Out and reduces Accounts Payable - never a new expense.
    Supplier payment = Dr Accounts Payable / Cr Cash. The inventory cost was already recognized at purchase time, so paying the supplier does not create another expense.
    Mao ni ang bayad sa utang sa supplier. Cash gawas, AP mokunhod; dili ni bag-ong expense.
