    Owner Dashboard

    Transparent Business View
    Kini nga section gi-design para sa owner decision-making: Cash & Liquidity = cash position, Profitability = business result, ug Financial Position = assets, liabilities, equity, receivables, inventory ug loans. Para dili magdoble-doble, kada card adunay usa ra ka primary destination sa Reports.
    Click any card to drill down to the supporting transactions in Reports.

    Recent Business Activity

    Latest recorded business events. “View” shows the source transaction and its accounting effect; loan records also show liquidation/allocation and summary.

    Cash Transactions

    Use Sales, Purchases and Collections modules for linked business events. Manual cash is for legitimate items such as expenses, owner capital, loans, drawings and other cash movements.

    Inventory

    Inventory uses historical transaction costs and weighted-average costing for new purchases and sales. Expiry is tracked per lot/batch. Expired stock is flagged for spoilage and is never silently removed.

    System Stocktake Snapshot

    Spoilage / Wastage

    Kung madaot, malata, malaya, o dili na baligya ang prutas/gulay, i-record diri. Ang system mo-minus sa inventory gamit ang weighted-average cost ug mo-record og separate Spoilage / Wastage Expense.
    Ang spoilage dili i-count as sales ug dili pud siya i-add sa cash outflow. Inventory loss siya nga na-convert ngadto sa Spoilage / Wastage Expense.

    Customer Collections / Receivables

    A collection reduces Accounts Receivable; it does NOT create new revenue. Link a collection to an actual credit sale whenever possible.

    Danger Zone — Wipe All Business Data

    This permanently deletes products, sales, purchases, collections, cash transactions, inventory movements, stocktakes, accounting effects and audit history. Your owner login, White-label settings, Receipt settings and branding assets are preserved.

    This cannot be undone. Create a full .vegebak backup before using this.
